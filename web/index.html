    3 · flash

    Cross-flashing is unsupported. Field units use different NOR flash types and densities. Publishing should warn about incompatibility, data loss, bricking, and warranty void.

    Teenage Engineering EP-series guidance on field NOR variance (Jul 2026) — paraphrased; not a marketing endorsement.

    Lab note: legacy EP-133 boards often use Winbond W25Q512JV (64 MiB); newer units advertise 128 MB sample pools.

    App firmware is MCUboot-encrypted — we cannot patch density tables from the host. See the write-up / disclosure timeline.
